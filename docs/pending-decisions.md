# Pending decisions

Proposals from the 28 Oct 2026 playtest. None of these are built yet; each needs a go-ahead.

## What the playtest showed

| Day | Takings | Target | Multiple |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Mon | $96 | $12 | 8× |
| Tue | $137 | $28 | 4.9× |
| Wed | $159 | $52 | 3× |
| Thu | $242 | $85 | 2.8× |
| Fri | $219 raw ($460 after critic ×2.1) | $130 | 1.7× raw |

- Targets are far too low; takings barely grow across the week while targets start tiny.
- The 1.5× star was earned every day, and bonus stars (big tipper almost every shift, influencers) gave 21★ against a goal of about 15★.
- Turbo grill's faster burning doesn't feel good.
- The Dishwasher relic (1 plate per 6s) couldn't keep up.
- Spoilage can be dodged entirely by building a menu without lettuce and tomato.

## Proposals

1. **Difficulty setting** chosen at the start of a run:

   | Day | Easy | Normal | Hard |
   | --- | --- | --- | --- |
   | Mon | $30 | $50 | $65 |
   | Tue | $50 | $80 | $105 |
   | Wed | $65 | $110 | $140 |
   | Thu | $90 | $150 | $195 |
   | Fri | $100 | $170 | $220 |

   Hard could also turn off match hints and shorten patience a little.

2. **Stop and ask when all stars are earned.** Once 1.3× the target is reached and the objective is currently met, pause with "All stars earned! Close up now or keep serving?" Closing locks in the stars (plus the bonus star with 20s+ left); keep serving continues without asking again.
3. **Second star at 1.3× the target** instead of 1.5×.
4. **At most 1 bonus star per shift** (big tipper, influencer or closing early).
5. **Dishwashing as a different action:** scrub back and forth on the sink (2 strokes per plate). Alternatives: press and hold 1s, or drag a dirty plate into the sink.
6. **Plates start on Tuesday**, replacing spoilage as Tuesday's new mechanic; spoilage moves later.
7. **Every ingredient has a shelf life:** lettuce and tomato fast, egg and cheese medium, bread slow, meat only if left very long. Optionally, produce dishes score a "fresh bonus".
8. **Turbo grill:** cooks faster, but grease flare-ups happen twice as often instead of faster burning.
9. **Dishwasher relic:** one plate every 3s instead of 6s, and 1 scrub per plate while owned.
